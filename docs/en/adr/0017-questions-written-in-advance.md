# ✍️ ADR 0017 — Interview questions are written in advance; the AI only reviews answers

> 🇫🇷 French version: [fr/adr/0017](../../fr/adr/0017-questions-ecrites-a-l-avance.md)

- **Date:** 2026-10-04
- **Status:** ✅ accepted — **supersedes** [ADR 0011](0011-one-question-set.md) (the question set is no longer generated by the AI)

## 🎯 Context

Questions were generated by one AI call per interview ([ADR 0011](0011-one-question-set.md)). With free models this was slow (up to 90 s), sometimes failed, and the French read like a word-for-word translation. A language detector tried to catch questions in the wrong language and misfired. The owner asked for a large bank of **typical HR questions, their variants and the ethically dubious or illegal ones**, written once by a strong model, and for the AI to be used only where it adds value: reviewing the candidate's own answers.

## ✅ Decision

- Questions come from **banks written in advance**, in real English and real French, each with its **model answer**:
  - `lib/interview/hr-bank/`: HR questions by type (introduction, motivation, strengths, conflict, salary… and inappropriate ones), with phrasing variants and the formal / informal French register (`{vous|tu}`);
  - `lib/interview/bank/`: technical questions for 42 technologies, grouped in tracks (`lib/interview/tracks.ts`).
- `buildQuestions` (`lib/interview/generate.ts`) picks them **by code**: a plan of slots per focus, the offer's verified stack or the practice topic, the candidate's facts, skill gaps, and questions not asked before.
- The AI is called **only for feedback** on an answer (`lib/interview/feedback.ts`), with the model answer as reference. The same answer to the same question reuses the saved feedback.
- The language detector is removed.

## 📊 Consequences

**Good** 👍

- Starting an interview is instant, free and never fails.
- Questions and model answers read naturally in both languages, and can be reviewed like any text.
- Interviews without an offer (a technology, HR only) come for free.

**Bad** 👎

- Questions are only as varied as the banks: adding a technology means writing its questions and answers.
- Questions are no longer tailored word by word to one offer; they follow its stack and gaps.
